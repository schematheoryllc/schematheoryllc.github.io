Our Services

At Schema Theory, we offer a full suite of enterprise consulting services designed to support data-driven strategy and execution. Our solutions span the full SDLC and are tailored to support high-performance analytics environments.

Data Architecture & Warehousing

Enterprise-scale schema and pipeline design for analytics-ready environments, supporting both cloud and hybrid deployments.

Business Intelligence Delivery

Dashboards, KPIs, and reports built in Power BI and Strategy (MicroStrategy), with reusable models and scalable performance.

Performance Optimization

SQL tuning, high-volume workload troubleshooting, and system audits to resolve latency and scale issues.

Analytics Engineering

Custom DAX measures, data modeling, tabular structures, and semantic layers that power consistent enterprise reporting.

Predictive & AI Solutions

ML models, classification, regression, and language models integrated into dashboards or data workflows.

Embedded Analytics & Portals

Secure embedded reporting and portal development using Row-Level Security (RLS), SSO, and identity-aware content delivery.

Data Strategy & Requirements

Agile requirements capture, product roadmapping, and stakeholder alignment for BI, analytics, and reporting projects.

Federal Data & Compliance

Support for government data platforms with experience in FedRAMP, FISMA, and high-volume regulatory workloads.

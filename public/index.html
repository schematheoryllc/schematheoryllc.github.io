Welcome to Schema Theory LLC, an information technology and business intelligence consulting company. Our services span the full system development life cycle (SDLC) including requirements definition, design, configuration and development testing, implementation, training, and maintenance of both custom and commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) solutions.

We specialize in data warehouse design and implementation in Oracle, Microsoft SQL Server, and cloud-based data sources with a focus on efficient relational schemas. Our Business Intelligence practice is supports the Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) and Microsoft Power BI product suites.

Our certifications include:

  • Project Management Institute (PMI) Certified Project Management Professional (PMP)
  • Microsoft Certified Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300)
  • Strategy ONE Certified Professional
  • Strategy Certified AI BI Professional
  • Strategy Certified Database Administrator

With our proven implementation methodology, we help customers manage scope, time, and cost while ensuring that all requirements and stakeholder needs are met on time and within budget.

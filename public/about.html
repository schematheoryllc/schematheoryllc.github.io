About Schema Theory LLC

At Schema Theory LLC, we pride ourselves on over eight years of expertise in systems integration, technical development, and project management. Our company's journey began under the leadership of our President, Michael Smereka, a seasoned professional with a rich background in both government agencies and private industry.

Our Expertise

Our expertise at Schema Theory LLC encompasses the full spectrum of the systems development life cycle (SDLC), ranging from requirements definition to design, configuration, development, testing, implementation, training, and maintenance. We specialize in business intelligence (BI), data science, data warehousing, performance measurement, and management.

Innovative Solutions

We have a track record of providing innovative solutions to government and private industry clients. Our company was pivotal in creating real-time geospatial dashboards, predictive analytics, and comprehensive dossiers serving over 400 locations nationwide. We have a commitment to delivering cutting-edge and impactful solutions to clients of any size and budget.

Recognized Excellence

Our excellence has not gone unnoticed. Members of our team have achieved notable accolades, including awards from The Data Warehouse Institute (TDWI) and recognition as a finalist in the ComputerWorld Best Practices in Business Intelligence. These achievements reflect our dedication to quality and excellence in our field.

Comprehensive Services

We offer a comprehensive range of services, including overseeing software development and integration, system architecture design, and the transformation of vast data sets. We specialize in large data warehouse design and optimization, including the population and analysis of billions of rows of data. We have showcased our capability to handle large-scale, complex projects across multiple Federal government contracts.

Our Mission

At Schema Theory LLC, our mission is to deliver exceptional technical solutions that drive performance and efficiency. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction remains at the core of everything we do.

Our Future

Looking ahead, we continue to embrace new challenges and opportunities, leveraging our vast experience and expertise to benefit our clients and the broader community.

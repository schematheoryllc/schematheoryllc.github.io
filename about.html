About Schema Theory

At Schema Theory, we pride ourselves on over twenty years of expertise in systems integration, technical development, and project management. Our company was founded by Michael Smereka, a seasoned professional with a rich background in both government agencies and private industry.

Our Expertise

Our capabilities encompass the full systems development life cycle (SDLC), including requirements definition, design, configuration, development, testing, implementation, training, and maintenance. We specialize in business intelligence (BI), data science, data warehousing, performance measurement, and enterprise management.

Innovative Solutions

We have a strong track record of providing innovative solutions to government and private industry clients. Our past projects include real-time geospatial dashboards, predictive analytics platforms, and nationwide reporting suites serving over 400 locations. Our solutions are built to deliver impact at any scale.

Recognized Excellence

Our team’s achievements include industry recognition such as awards from The Data Warehouse Institute (TDWI) and finalist honors in the ComputerWorld Best Practices in Business Intelligence. These accolades reflect our dedication to delivering excellence and innovation.

Comprehensive Services

We deliver end-to-end technical services, from architecture and software development to large-scale system integration. Our experience includes the design and population of data warehouses with billions of rows, performance optimization, and execution across complex federal contracts.

Our mission: To deliver exceptional technical solutions that drive performance and efficiency. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do.

Looking Ahead

As we continue to grow, we remain committed to tackling new challenges and delivering value to our clients through thoughtful, results-driven solutions.
